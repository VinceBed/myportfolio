Contact. For research collaborations, mentorship, speaking, and press enquiries.

Get in touch

I am glad to hear from researchers, students, journalists, and policymakers working on questions of demography, climate, and population health, and from organisations exploring collaboration in these areas. The fastest route is email; I aim to reply within a week, with longer delays during conference periods and end-of-semester teaching obligations.

The most reliable way to reach me is email. Please include a brief description of your enquiry and any relevant deadlines in the first message.

What I am open to

  • Research collaborations on climate and population health, health inequalities, or spatial demography in sub-Saharan Africa.
  • Co-authorship discussions and manuscript-review enquiries within my areas of expertise.
  • Mentorship conversations with early-career researchers in demography, population health, and applied statistics, with a particular openness to colleagues from Ghana and the broader West African region.
  • Speaking and panel invitations on climate change and maternal–perinatal health, ethnic and geographic health disparities, and reproducible computational practice in the social sciences.
  • Press and media enquiries on demography, population health, and the Ghanaian context.
  • Consulting on quantitative methods for demographic analysis, geospatial linkage of survey data, and reproducible R-based research pipelines.

A note on response

I aim to reply to substantive email enquiries within a week. Where a longer delay is unavoidable, I appreciate a polite follow-up after seven to ten days. For urgent press enquiries with a same-day deadline, please mark the subject line accordingly.

I am unable to provide individualised statistical consulting outside of formal collaborations or institutional channels. For graduate students seeking advice on dissertation methods, I respond to specific, well-defined questions but cannot serve as a long-term informal supervisor outside my own institution.
