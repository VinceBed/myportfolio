Featured

Hidden by national averages: ethnic disparities in under-five survival across sub-Saharan Africa

Bediako, V. B. (2025). Hidden by national averages: Unveiling the complex dynamics of ethnic disparities in under-five survival across sub-Saharan Africa. SSM-Population Health, 29, 101870.

This article re-examines under-five mortality across fourteen sub-Saharan African countries, decomposing national averages to expose ethnic and geographic heterogeneity that aggregate statistics obscure. The analysis combines survey-weighted multilevel modelling with sensitivity tests for the role of administrative geography, demonstrating that ethnic disparities are partially — but unevenly — mediated by location, with implications for how disparity statistics are reported and interpreted in population health surveillance.
